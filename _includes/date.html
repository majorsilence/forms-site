{%- comment -%} Formats a date in the current language. Usage: {% include date.html date=post.date %} Liquid's `date` filter only knows English month names, so the month comes from site.data.i18n[lang].months and the order from its date_format (%D day, %M month name, %Y year). Depends on `t` from lang-vars.html. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign d_month_index = include.date | date: "%-m" | plus: 0 | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign d_month = t.months[d_month_index] -%} {%- assign d_day = include.date | date: "%-d" -%} {%- assign d_year = include.date | date: "%Y" -%} {{- t.date_format | replace: "%D", d_day | replace: "%M", d_month | replace: "%Y", d_year -}}
